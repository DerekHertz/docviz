V · what the prototype says

What it settled, and what it didn't

  1. The picture stays the same size however big the PR is. 1,079 lines became 8 states and 11 arrows in plain words, and the one thing to look at is in red.
  2. TLC found a real gap that has survived to main. It took seconds and cost nothing, and your two sentences were the only spec it needed.
  3. Jev can't confirm the bug itself (the late-return step scored 0.19). The bug is a throw caught three lines later. TLC finds the path; a person or Claude confirms it in the code.
  4. Jev passes a step only when its whole claim fits in the linked code. Retry (→ generate), Simpler (→ learn) and dismiss-while-running cross functions or depend on timing, and went to review. Each model step should link every function it passes through, and the TLA+ action already lists them.
  5. "Skip Claude when nothing changed" is untested. No step passed at both base and head, because this PR changed nearly everything it touched. It needs a PR that leaves most steps alone.
  6. Left out of the model: pictures, Explore, the breakdown's batches, concurrent cards, the soft cap's overshoot. A redraw always ends at "budget used up" because the budget is only 2 calls.
